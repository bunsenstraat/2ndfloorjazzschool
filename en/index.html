Saxophone lessons

From your first note to your own sound

Lessons for children and adults on alto, tenor and soprano sax. Beginners learn the basics — holding the horn, breathing, tone, reading music — in about ten lessons. Experienced players work on tone, timing, repertoire, theory and improvisation.

  • 40- or 60-minute private lessons
  • Lessons in English or Dutch
  • Advice on buying or renting a saxophone
  • Coaching for bands and big band sax sections
Jazz workshops

Learn to play together

The 2nd Floor Jazz School runs small, regular groups working on jazz standards, original tunes and world music: ensemble playing, rhythm and improvising over chord changes. Piano, double bass, drums and amps are in the studio. Every season ends with a concert.

Open to all instruments and levels — including players new to jazz.

About

Eldert van Oosten

Amsterdam-born saxophonist, trained at the Sweelinck Conservatory (improvising musician programme, graduated 1988). Taught at the Leiden music school for 22 years before opening his own studio in Amsterdam-West in 2010. Has played with Eye of the Hurricane, De Dijk, the Ricciotti Ensemble, the Sidewinders and the Belleville Trio, among many others.
