Jazz workshops

Learn to play together

The 2nd Floor Jazz School runs small, regular groups working on jazz standards, blues, latin and original tunes: ensemble playing, rhythm and improvising over chord changes. Every season ends with a concert.

For adult musicians on any instrument who have some jazz experience. You don’t need to be an experienced improviser — that’s what the group is for — but a basic grounding in jazz is needed to take part. Not sure? Get in touch and we’ll talk about your level.

  • €20per person per workshop
  • 90 minper workshop
  • Mon · Tue · Wedtime by arrangement with Eldert
Saxophone lessons

From your first note to your own sound

Private lessons for adults on alto, tenor and soprano sax with Eldert. Beginners learn the basics — holding the horn, breathing, tone, reading music. Experienced players work on tone, timing, repertoire, theory and improvisation.

  • 40- or 60-minute private lessons
  • Lessons in English or Dutch
  • A route into the jazz workshops
  • Advice on buying or renting a saxophone
Your teacher

Eldert van Oosten

Amsterdam-born jazz saxophonist, trained at the Sweelinck Conservatory (improvising musician programme, graduated 1988). He taught at the Leiden music school for 22 years before starting the 2nd Floor Jazz School in Amsterdam-West in 2010. He has played with Eye of the Hurricane, De Dijk, the Ricciotti Ensemble, the Sidewinders and the Belleville Trio, among many others.
